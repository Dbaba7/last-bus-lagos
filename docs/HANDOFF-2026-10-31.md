## The story now
The opening order is: **prologue → "Chapter 01 · Signal Lost" card → Ada's opening at 18:31 → the existing intro from i1 → Signal Lost.**

Canon established during this work:
- **The transfer changed tonight.** Femi expected the seven o'clock (the last bus). The Sergeant put him on Danfo 247. "Plan changed." "Who changed it?" "Command." "Which command?" echoes Ada and Control in Chapter 2. Who changed it is unknown.
- **The Sergeant's identity is deliberately undecided.** Give reasons to distrust him, never a conclusion. He wears a dark jacket over a police-blue shirt, so the figure reflected in the Chapter 2 photo could be him or someone else. He leaves the bus before the attack and doesn't return.
- **The second phone.** He gives it to Femi: "If I'm not back, use this. Only this." It is the phone Control later pings (c5), and STATIC are at the press when Ada arrives. Was he saving Femi or tagging him? Never answer this yet.
- **The photo is Femi's.** He takes it on the bus before 18:40. It sticks on "Sending…". The picture must not be shown in the prologue, and the recipient stays hidden, so Chapter 2 is the reveal. The question is who received it and how. One idea, not committed: the phone resends when Ada restores Relay 07, so she delivers it unknowingly.
- **The jammed messages.** Femi drops his main phone on the bus. At the press, when the jam flickers for a second, he texts that phone from the second phone, because it's the one phone the police are sure to find. That is how a3 and a4 can be read.
- **The Corporal is the Unit Seven voice,** so his later "Ada! GET DOWN!" and "the target wasn't supposed to be—" come from someone the player has met. He and the Sergeant have no names yet.
- **Ada never met Femi.** f1 says "Officer — thank God", so they can't meet before Chapter 1. She wrote his name in the file.
- **"Who signs off the outages"** is the question that got Ada sent to a checkpoint. "Eleven" is the outage count everywhere.
- **Never reveal** what's on the drive, who paid for the jammer, or what the witness list is.

## Remaining milestones

### Milestone 3A: ambience and radio (next)
The aim: the player experiences an ordinary Lagos evening, then it empties out at 18:40. The street currently plays the dead-junction or restored ambience, so neither Ada's 18:31 scene nor the park sounds alive yet.

1. **`ambience()` must crossfade.** It currently stops one mood and starts another (`audio.ts`). Make layers fade independently.
2. **New `live` mood:** market chatter, heavier traffic, street voices, hawker calls. Used while the `live` flag is set and in Ada's scene (`fx: 'live'`). In `main.ts` the `live` flag is mapped to the `restored` mood as a stopgap. The existing `dusk` mood is the dead junction Signal Lost starts in, so it must stay as it is.
3. **Crowd walla:** synthesized speech-like crowd sound rarely convinces, so a short recorded pack may be worth it. Ask the author.
4. **Soundtrack module:** new `apps/client/src/soundtrack.ts`, separate from `voice.ts` and the sound effects, with a track list in data: `{ id, title, artist, credit, src?, placeholder, licensed, cueIn, cueOut, unlock }`.
5. **Placeholder track:** a generated, original 90-second loop of percussion, bass and guitar, going through the same playback path as a real file, so a licensed file replaces it with no change to the scene.
6. **Licensed files stay out of the repo** until a licence is signed: `apps/client/src/music/`, in `.gitignore`. A missing file falls back to the placeholder silently. Stream through an audio element rather than decoding a whole song, because that's too heavy for phones. Fetch only when needed.
7. **Routing:** track → radio speaker filter (tinny) → signal stage (static, dropouts, distortion) → world position (the dashboard) with the bus interior echo → soundtrack bus → Music volume → Master. Music keeps ducking under speech. News and adverts (`p8`, `p11`) use the same speaker sound on a new `radio` channel.
8. **Cutscene cue:** a state cue `{ radio: 'play' | 'stop', station?, level?, signal?, muffled?, seconds? }`, so skipping leaves the radio correct. Muffled in exterior shots, clear in interior ones.
9. **New setting:** "Licensed music" (on or off), which swaps to the placeholder for streamers.
10. **The 18:40 sequence:** radio order on the bus is news, advert, then the song, with only 45–60 seconds of the song heard. Then:

| Time | Sound | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| Before | Song on the radio, horns, market, conductor, engine | Femi's photo stuck on "Sending…" |
| −6 s | Phone interference buzz through the radio speaker | Femi looks at his phone |
| −3 s | Buzz spreads; bars start falling | Billboard flickers |
| 0 s (18:40) | Music breaks up, pitch wobbles, static rises | Relay lights red, POS fails, NO SIGNAL |
| +1.5 s | Song cuts to hiss, then a dead click | Driver slaps the radio |
| +2 to +6 s | Passengers laugh; "Na network o" | Everyone lifts a phone |
| +6 to +12 s | Market and traffic thin out, horns fade, a low hum rises | Femi says "All the bands" |
| +12 s | Almost nothing but engine and the hum | Bikes appear, the bus slows |
| Stop | Engine dies, footsteps, near silence | The side door |

The prologue ends in the `dusk` mood so it sounds exactly like the start of Signal Lost.

11. **Small payoff:** Danfo 247's dead radio hissing quietly when Ada searches it, with no new recording.
12. **Tests:** a missing track falls back to the placeholder; skipping leaves the radio off and the ambience right; retrying doesn't restart the song; the streamer setting swaps the track; playback stops on leaving a mission.
13. **Update `docs/assets.md`,** which says the game has no commercial music.

### Milestone 7: licence and soundtrack (runs alongside)
The author wants "Ojuelegba" by Wizkid on Danfo 247's radio. **Never embed or redistribute copyrighted music without licences.** Don't put its lyrics on screen. A commercial recording normally needs two permissions, the master and the composition; identify the current rights holders first. Ask for interactive game use, territories and term, marketing use, soundtrack-player use and streamer terms. A 45–60 second excerpt may be much cheaper. If it falls through, commission an original Lagos-style track; no recorded line may name the song. An optional Soundtrack page lists only tracks licensed for it and stays hidden if there are none. A lawyer should check the deal.

## Things that will trip you up
- **The dev server stops between sessions.** Start it with `preview_start` (`dev` from `.claude/launch.json`). If port 5173 is taken, another session's server is serving the same files, and you can use it. One tour failed because the page hadn't got its menu after a build; check the server before suspecting the game.
- **World state waits for queued scenes.** `tick()` holds `syncWorld` and the relay while `story.cutscenes` isn't empty. So flags set by an objective apply after its scenes, and a scene shows the earlier state until a cue changes it.
- **A skipped scene drops one-off cues** (`sound`, `fx`, `caption`…). `endCinema` restores the city with `cityState()`. Anything else a scene changes through a one-off cue needs the same care.
- **Character costs about 12 draw calls.** Bystanders only appear within 20 m for this reason. The frozen crowd is one draw call but looks bad close up, so keep it out of walking paths.
- **Line up new scene and walk positions with collision.** Use `tests/unit/story.test.ts`'s camera test, and walk the route in `pursuit.test.ts` style. A market stall silently blocked the first park walk.
- **Hunter rules are opt-in** through `Mission.pursuit`; Signal Lost and multiplayer are untouched. Keep it that way. `VERSION` is still `lagos-3`.
- **Bash heredocs containing backticks can fail** in this environment. Write patch scripts to a file with the Write tool and run them.
- **The traversal test is slow** (about 5 s) and has a 20 s limit for that reason.
