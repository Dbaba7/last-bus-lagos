# LAST BUS: LAGOS — work log and handoff (28–29 October 2026)

This records everything done in the 0.2 upgrade session, so a new session can continue without re-deriving it. Code and other docs: `README.md`, `docs/dev-log.md`, `docs/voice-script.md`.

## Project at a glance

- **Stack (unchanged):** TypeScript npm workspaces, Node 24. Client: Vite + Babylon.js 9 (`apps/client`). Server: Colyseus 0.18 + Express (`apps/server`). Shared rules and simulation: `packages/shared`.
- **Run:** in `last-bus-lagos-main/`, `npm ci`, then `npm run dev`. The client is http://localhost:5173 and the game server is :2567 (the dev script pins the server to 2567 even when a launcher sets `PORT`).
- **Protocol version:** `lagos-2`. Bump it whenever the input contract, snapshot fields or map geometry change.
- The local folder is **not a git repo**; nothing has been committed. The private GitHub repo `Qudios/last-bus-lagos` holds the older 0.1 code only.

## What was built, in order

### 1. Audit and baseline
Read the whole codebase (about 99 KB of dense TypeScript). The baseline built and passed 28 tests. The project was already server-authoritative, with prediction and interpolation, and a single small box-arena map.

### 2. Shared rules (`packages/shared/src`)
- **Teams:** renamed **Police (0) vs Bandits (1)**; winners are `POLICE` / `BANDITS` (`TEAMS` in `config.ts`).
- **Movement:** crouch (1.2 m body; can't stand under ceilings), aim-down-sights speed, air control, separate deceleration, and a **0.45 m step-up** for kerbs and stairs (`physics.ts`).
- **Weapons:** data-driven (`WEAPONS` in `config.ts`) — designed recoil patterns with recovery, a **deterministic per-shot spread** shared by prediction and server, damage falloff, headshots, auto-reload on an empty trigger pull, and a longer empty reload (`weapons.ts`).
- **Input:** two new fields, `crouch` and `aim`; older clients that omit them still parse.
- **Private rooms:** hosts can set match length and elimination limit; players can switch teams while waiting. Both are validated server-side (`simulation.ts`, `GameRoom.ts`, schema fields).
- **Story options on the simulation:** no auto-respawn, regeneration for the player team only (configurable delay and rate), a per-shooter damage scale, and `melee()` cutlass strikes (solo modes only).

### 3. Map: Ojuelegba Junction (`packages/shared/src/world.ts`)
- **Size and symmetry:** rebuilt at 80 × 64 m. The south half is authored and the north half is a 180° rotation with its own names, colours and dressing.
- **Layout:**
  - A flyover deck with pillars, kerbed pavements and a central junction.
  - Two walk-through shops: Unity Electricals in the south and Kola Printing Press in the north.
  - A Junction Records / Adunni Hotel stair-and-balcony on each side, the Ade & Sons mechanic yard, and the Signal 9 telecom yard with a relay cabinet and mast.
  - A police checkpoint plaza in the south and a Danfo Park in the north.
  - Real buildings and fences as the boundary, and trees with collision.
- **Collision and visuals:** visuals are built from the same boxes as collision. `TEST_LANE` (x = 7) is kept clear for the multiplayer tests.

### 4. Rendering (`apps/client/src/render`, `world.ts`, `renderer.ts`)
- **Textures:** procedural, tileable textures with normal maps (concrete, plaster, asphalt, paving, corrugated metal and more); the tile size is baked into the UVs and textures are shared.
- **Geometry:** static geometry is batched per material (about 100–200 draw calls).
- **Signage:** a single **4096² sign atlas** for all signs, posters, danfo stickers and billboards.
- **Lighting and effects:** dusk lighting, a sun shadow map baked once, ACES tone mapping, FXAA and bloom, and a sky dome. Ambient animation covers ceiling fans, swaying trees, beacons, a failing fluorescent tube and distant traffic.
- **Characters** (`characters.ts`):
  - Articulated, vertex-coloured rigs with procedural gait, strafe, crouch, aim, reload, flinch and death.
  - Looks: Police in navy with vests; Bandits with **red scarves**; civilians (Femi). Cutlass variants with a swing animation.
  - Fixed: disposing an actor used to destroy the shared material, which made every later character invisible.
- **First-person view** (`viewmodel.ts`): arms parented to the weapon; ADS, sprint, equip and reload choreography; spring recoil; muzzle flash. Reload sound cues come from the animation.
- **Effects** (`effects.ts`): pooled tracers, impacts by surface, bullet-hole decals and brass.

### 5. Audio (`audio.ts`, `voice.ts`)
- **Synthesis:** everything is synthesized on 5 buses (master, effects, ambience, music, dialogue) with HRTF spatialization. Sounds: guns, reload parts, footsteps by surface, landing, hit and kill confirmation, cutlass slash, layered dusk ambience, and a light menu and combat score.
- **Recorded voice:** files in `apps/client/src/voice/` (`.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.wav` or `.ogg`) are bundled through `import.meta.glob`. All **28 lines are recorded by the user and verified to decode.** CONTROL lines go through a handset-style radio filter, and subtitles stay up for the length of each recording.

### 6. UI (`ui/markup.ts`, `ui/settingsView.ts`, `styles.css`, `main.ts`)
- **Screens:**
  - Menu with a live 3D dolly background, keyboard navigation, real server status and a global top bar (callsign, settings).
  - Story (chapters, mission card, difficulty, checkpoint/continue), Multiplayer (quick match, private room, join by code), Training and a tabbed Settings dialog.
  - Briefing, loading, lobby (8 slots, ready/ping, host match settings, switch team, invite), pause (mode-aware, with confirmations), mission end (stats) and match results.
- **Settings:** separate X and Y sensitivity, ADS multiplier, FOV, invert, hold or toggle aim, crouch and sprint, **key rebinding**, quality, shadows, render scale, post effects, five volumes, camera shake, bob, subtitle size, HUD scale, difficulty and a performance overlay.
- **Desktop HUD:** compass with objective and threat markers, a 3D objective marker with distance, a crosshair that widens with the real spread, hit markers (head and kill), damage-direction arcs, interaction prompts with a hold ring, subtitles, kill feed, scoreboard, and a diagnostics overlay (F3).
- **Touch HUD (latest):** round icon buttons drawn for this game; a joystick with chevrons and a yellow knob; left and right Fire; Aim, Crouch, Jump and Reload; Use only when available; a weapon tray with ammo; a radar (solo: aware enemies and the objective; online: teammates only); pause and scores; and a "turn your phone sideways" overlay in portrait.
- **Responsive:** interface zoom of 1–2.6× above 1600×900, plus compact layouts for narrow and short screens and notch safe areas. Checked by `scripts/responsive-audit.mjs` (10 sizes × 12 screens).

### 7. Story Mode (`packages/shared/src/story`)
- **Campaign "Dead Air":** Chapter 01 **"Signal Lost"** is playable; chapters 02 and 03 are shown as *in development*.
- **Story:** you play Inspector Ada Nwosu. STATIC (fictional) jam the networks to attack the witness danfo carrying Femi Adeyemi.
- **Eight objectives:** reach the junction, search the danfo, clear the Signal 9 yard, pull the jammer, hold the yard for 45 s, find Femi in Kola Printing Press, clear Danfo Park, then get Femi onto the last bus (escort).
- **Data-driven missions** (`campaign.ts`): objectives, interactables, encounters, dialogue lines with `direction` notes for actors, checkpoints and **cutscenes** (camera shots: `from`, `to`, `look`, `lookTo`, `seconds`, `line`, `fov`; use the helper `C(x, y, z)` — the mission helper `P(x, z, y)` takes a different argument order).
- **AI** (`ai.ts`):
  - States: patrol, suspicious, investigate, engage, cover, flank, reload, search. Perception is line-of-sight only, with hearing and ally alerts.
  - Combat: reaction delay, aim error that settles over time, bursts, cover selection and stuck recovery.
  - Behaviour: roles guard, patrol, holder, rusher, flanker and bot, plus **`melee: true` cutlass carriers** (they stop at arm's length).
- **Difficulty** (`DIFFICULTY` in `ai.ts`): accuracy, damage, enemy health, burst and pause timing, sight, awareness, **roster share** (Easy ≈ 50% of each group, Normal 75%, Hard 100%), **patience** before pushing, rush distance, cutlass damage, and player regeneration (Easy heals after 2.5 s at 45 health per second).
- **Session** (`session.ts`):
  - Objective manager, checkpoints (a retry rebuilds a fresh session), held interactions and ammo resupply.
  - Reinforcements arrive one by one, 3.5 s apart, with a "Hostiles incoming from the …" notice.
  - Femi follows you, staying seated until he first moves. Enemy barks have stable keys, and bodies are cleaned up after 8 s.
- **Cutscenes (client):** letterboxed, with the HUD hidden and the session frozen. **Click** for the next line, **hold Space** to skip the scene, **Enter or Esc** to skip. Four exist: intro (3 shots), relay (1), femi (2) and outro (2).
- **Training:** six targets at measured distances, or three bots that roam without cheating.

### 8. Tooling and tests
- **Tests:** `npm test` runs 51 unit tests, including a scripted run of the whole mission, AI line-of-sight, retry state, cutscene queue, difficulty roster, cutlass and regeneration. `npm run test:integration` runs 11 tests. `test:multiplayer`, `test:e2e` and `test:browser-combat` are updated for the new UI and pass.
- **Scripts:**
  - `scripts/visual-tour.mjs` (dev screenshot walkthrough) and `scripts/responsive-audit.mjs` (layout audit).
  - `npm run voice:script` regenerates `docs/voice-script.md`.
- **Dev only:** `window.lastBusDev` (teleport, skip, clearEnemies, heal, god, kill, look, inspect) and F4/F8/F9 keys. None of these exist in production builds.
- **Headless Chrome tip:** use `PLAYWRIGHT_CHROMIUM_EXECUTABLE="C:/Program Files/Google/Chrome/Application/chrome.exe"` with `--use-angle=d3d11` for real GPU screenshots. The in-app browser pane throttles rendering when unfocused, so don't trust its frame rate.

## Known gaps and honest status
- **Not verified:** real Android or iPhone hardware, public hosting (Render not deployed), and internet multiplayer.
- **Not run since 0.1:** latency profiles and the 30-minute soak. `test:browser-recovery` still targets the 0.1 settings layout and needs a rewrite.
- **Art:** everything is procedural low-poly (no imported models). Faces are simple, and characters have no skinned meshes.
- **AI navigation** is ground-level only; balcony enemies are stationary "holders".
- **Cutscenes** are camera-only: characters don't act in them yet (no staged positions or animations), there are no cuts or transitions beyond moves between shots, and there is no depth of field or letterbox fade.
- **Mission load** takes about 0.3–0.5 s of nav-grid build (now pre-built while on the menu).

## Phase 1: traversal (`packages/shared/src/physics.ts`)
- Vault, mantle, ladder climb and slide are part of `movePlayer`. The server simulates them and client prediction replays them identically. `Player` carries `traverse`, `traverseT`, `from*`, `to*` and `ledgeY`, and the schema syncs them.
- **No new input field:** jump into an obstacle vaults it or mantles onto it. In the air, pushing forward into a reachable ledge pulls you up. Walking into a ladder grabs it (forward/back to climb, jump to let go). Crouching while sprinting slides. With toggle crouch, or on touch, a crouch tap mid-sprint slides and then stands you up. `parseInput` is unchanged.
- **Rules are data:** `Box.traverse` is one of `auto`, `vault`, `climb` or `none`. Boxes without the flag get a default from their kind (`traversal()`). Buildings, shells, fences, pillars and the flyover are never traversable. A mantle can reach at most 2.05 m above the floor you stand on, and `WORLD_BOUNDS` keeps landings in the arena. A test sweeps every tall box face to prove no roof or out-of-bounds spot is reachable.
- **New routes:** a ladder on each yard container, and a drainpipe beside each records/hotel balcony. Each pipe ends in a vault over the railing.
- **Feel:** camera dip, roll and nod; slide lean and FOV kick; free first-person hands (a plant on vaults, both hands on mantles, alternating on ladders); third-person traversal poses; synthesized vault, mantle, rung and slide sounds. A context hint ("Space VAULT") sits under the crosshair, and on touch it shows on the jump button.
- **AI:** an agent pinned against low cover vaults it. Agents normally path around cover, so this is a fallback.

## Phase 2: cutscene director (`apps/client/src/cinema.ts`)
- Each `Cutscene` now has `cast` (staged actors) and timed `cues`: actor walk/turn/pose/gesture, prop path/door, fade, grade, letterbox, shake, tumble, sound, sting, duck, overlapping `say` lines and story effects.
- Each `Shot` has `ease`, `blend` (a move from the previous shot instead of a cut), `fovTo`, handheld `shake` and `roll`. Click still means next line, holding Space skips the scene, and recorded voice length still sets shot length.
- When a shot or scene is skipped, its state cues still land (actors, props, fades, grades); one-off sounds and effects are dropped. Story outcomes are session actions (`flag`, `remove`, `arm`), so skipping can never lose them.
- **Renderer:** colour grades (ColorCurves, always enabled), a dusk/night mood blend, the blast flash, pooled `Spectacle` effects (sky lights, streak, fireball, shockwave ring, dust, glass, smoke columns), a movable last-bus node with a sliding door, and a city state: dead, restored (lit windows and signs, extra traffic) or blackout.
- **Audio:** ambience ducks under any speech; there are ambience moods (dusk, restored, shelter), stings and cutscene sound design.
